Merapi Dawn Run is a STOL bush-pilot flight game: dawn over Java, a clinic on the far island out of medicine, and you are the only plane that can thread the volcano pass. Fly the amber rings across three legs, terrace valley, the smoking Merapi pass, and the strait crossing, mind your fuel and airframe, and put her down gently on three short strips.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Fly with arrow keys, throttle with W and S, Shift to boost, Space to brake, P to pause, R to retry a leg, M to mute.
